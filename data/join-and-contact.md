# Join the lab and contact us

## PhD opportunities
Prospective doctoral candidates can apply for research degrees in any of the
lab's five research themes, supervised within the Peter Faber Business School.

## Research assistant roles
Current students and graduates can apply for short-term research assistant roles
supporting data work, evaluation studies and prototype development.

## Student placements and internships
ACU students can apply for industry placements and internships on lab projects,
including website development, data analysis and AI prototypes.

## Industry and government collaboration
Organisations and government bodies can work with the lab on joint projects,
pilots, student boot camps and applied research.

## Contact
Address: Peter Faber Business School, Australian Catholic University, North Sydney
NSW, Australia. Email and phone details are still to be confirmed on the website.
The website has a contact form for general enquiries, research collaboration,
industry partnerships and prospective research students.
