# Research themes

The AIDX Lab organises its work into five research themes.

## 1. Decision Intelligence and Human-AI Collaboration
Advancing systems that help people make better decisions, with humans and AI
working together rather than AI replacing human judgement.

## 2. Trustworthy AI and Governance
Building AI that is responsible, explainable, fair and accountable, and studying
how organisations should govern it.

## 3. Agentic AI and Adaptive Systems
Developing intelligent agents that can reason, adapt and collaborate safely. This
includes self-improving LLM systems and how agent memory works in production
(see the "Human Layer of Agentic AI Memory" chapter).

## 4. AI for Learning and Capability Development
Using AI to improve education, assessment and personalised learning, for example
LLM-based teaching agents, adaptive nudging for student success, and dynamic
personalisation of learning styles.

## 5. AI for Organisations and Society
Applying AI to create measurable impact across business, education, government
and communities, such as energy forecasting and health applications.
