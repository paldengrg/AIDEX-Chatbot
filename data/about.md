# About the AIDX Lab

## Who we are
The AIDX Lab (AI for Decision Excellence) is a research lab within the Peter Faber
Business School at Australian Catholic University (ACU), based on the North Sydney
campus, NSW, Australia.

## Mission
The lab's purpose is to design, evaluate and govern artificial intelligence that
improves important decisions.

## Core principles
Every project in the lab is built around five principles: safety, fairness,
privacy, accountability and explainability.

## Interdisciplinary approach
The lab brings together expertise from six disciplines: artificial intelligence,
business, law, ethics, education and information systems.

## Online presence
The lab website is https://aidxlab.github.io/. It also has a YouTube channel with
video demonstrations and project updates, and the lab's researchers have Google
Scholar and LinkedIn profiles linked from the website.
