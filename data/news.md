# News and events

## IT Boot Camp (May 2026)
In May 2026 the lab ran an IT Boot Camp in partnership with TechSkills Institute,
engaging nearly 80 ACU students. Topics included helpdesk ticketing, Entra ID,
Office 365, device management and interview preparation. Two students received
internship opportunities as a result. A video of the event is on the lab's YouTube
channel.
